\subsection{Datasets}
\label{subsec:datasets}

We construct multilingual bias evaluation benchmarks in English, Hindi, and Bengali by extending two established English-only datasets: (i)~\textbf{CrowS-Pairs}~\cite{nangia2020crows}, comprising 1,508 sentence pairs across nine bias categories (race, gender, religion, age, nationality, disability, physical appearance, sexual orientation, and socioeconomic status), and (ii)~\textbf{Indian Bias Evaluation Dataset} (Indian-BhED)~\cite{khandelwal2023indian}, containing 761 sentence pairs spanning four India-specific bias dimensions---caste, gender, religion, and race. For each dataset, we produce Hindi and Bengali translations through a three-phase pipeline. First, baseline translations are generated using Google Translate. These are then refined through a five-stage LLM post-editing pipeline that employs a four-model cascade with no per-model retries: \emph{Gemini~2.5 Flash Lite} (Google Gemini API) as the primary model, followed by \emph{DeepSeek Chat} (DeepSeek API), \emph{Gemini~3 } (Google Gemini API), and \emph{Qwen~Turbo} (OpenRouter API) as successive fallbacks. The pipeline stages are: (1)~\emph{LLM post-editing}, where the cascade refines initial machine translations to improve fluency while preserving \texttt{MASK} tokens and bias-related semantics; (2)~\emph{quality judging}, where the same cascade scores each translation on five dimensions---semantic fidelity, mask preservation, stereotype equivalence, grammatical naturalness, and pronoun-gender accuracy---yielding a verdict of \textsc{pass}, \textsc{needs\_revision}, or \textsc{fail}; (3)~\emph{re-refinement}, where sentences receiving \textsc{needs\_revision} are revised by the cascade incorporating judge feedback and then re-judged; (4)~\emph{fallback translation}, where sentences that still fail after Stage~3 are freshly translated by the cascade and re-judged, with the best-scoring attempt retained for persistently failing cases; and (5)~\emph{back-translation validation}, where all finalized translations are back-translated to English via the Google Cloud Translation API~v2---an independent system from the refinement LLMs---and evaluated using BERTScore~F1 and chrF++ as automated quality metrics. Finally, all translations undergo manual post-processing to correct residual errors.

To verify translation quality, we employ stratified sampling across bias categories and draw 200~samples per dataset (400~total). A human evaluator independently assesses each sampled sentence for semantic correctness, naturalness, and preservation of the intended stereotypical contrast, confirming that the automated pipeline produces reliable translations. The resulting multilingual benchmarks are publicly available on HuggingFace: \textbf{Multi-CrowS-Pairs}\footnote{\url{https://huggingface.co/datasets/Debk/Multi-CrowS-Pairs}} and \textbf{Indian Multilingual Bias Dataset}\footnote{\url{https://huggingface.co/datasets/Debk/Indian-Multilingual-Bias-Dataset}}.
